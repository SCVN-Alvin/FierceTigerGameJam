WHO THIS IS FOR
    The designer and the image generator producing concepts for Mission 2 and
    Mission 3, and the AI that will clone those concepts into map JSON. Every
    level below carries: the archetype, the grid, the material band, the
    "collapse story" (what makes destroying it feel good), and a ready-to-use
    IMAGE PROMPT. Concepts must be drawn at CELL RESOLUTION - one drawn brick
    = one 0.25 m cell of the stated grid - or the JSON cannot honour them.


================================================================================
1. WHY MISSION 1 READS FLAT - THE DIAGNOSIS THIS FIXES
================================================================================

    All nine current buildings are the same organism: a hollow rectangular
    box with window holes. Every map plays the same way - stand in front,
    sand the facade down, bricks drop at the foot of the wall. No shot is
    smarter than another, nothing chains, the structure never betrays itself.
    Demolition is only fun when the building has a STRUCTURE TO BETRAY: a
    load path the player can read, cut, and watch fail.

    Every level below is therefore built around one of five construction
    styles, and no level is a plain box.


================================================================================
2. THE FIVE CONSTRUCTION STYLES
================================================================================

    S1  PILLAR PUZZLE - a heavy mass carried by 2-4 slim pillars. The player
        reads "shoot the legs", cuts them, gravity does the rest. The engine
        already releases everything above a destroyed support.
        Shapes: water tower, elevated tank, grandstand, viaduct.

    S2  DEPTH DOMINO - 3-4 tall free-standing slabs placed one behind
        another, 1-2 layers apart. A knocked wall topples as one plate and
        slams the next hard enough to activate it: one good shot fells the
        whole row. Uses a chain mechanic the engine has that NO current map
        exercises.

    S3  COMPOUND / DIORAMA - two or three small separate structures on one
        map (main hall + chimney + shed). Every bullet becomes a target
        CHOICE, and a falling chimney can maul the building beside it.

    S4  CANTILEVER & ARCH - bridges, gates, overhangs propped by one column:
        shapes that look unstable and itch to be shot. Physics pays it off.

    S5  SHOT ECONOMY - not a shape but a tuning rule that makes S1-S4
        matter: set bulletPickLimit to ~60-70% of the shots needed to sand
        the building down, so passing REQUIRES structural shots.

    MATERIAL BANDS decide where the fun comes from (per mission of nine:
    levels 1-3 brick only, 4-6 brick+glass, 7-9 brick+glass+concrete):
        brick band    -> fun = shape and domino (no material contrast, so
                         silhouettes and chains carry everything)
        glass band    -> fun = glass rain: continuous panes riding on the
                         load-bearing band below them, released all at once
        concrete band -> fun = anatomy: visible skeleton, player learns to
                         punch the brick infill and then fell the frame


================================================================================
3. MISSION 2 - BEACH / SEASIDE FAMILY
================================================================================

Theme: a seaside strip - huts, piers, lifeguard towers, a lighthouse. The
materials do not change art, so the THEME IS CARRIED BY SHAPE and by how the
image generator paints the same three blocks: brick reads as sand-brick and
timber, glass as sea-view glazing, concrete as stone piers and breakwater.
Everything stands on ground or stilts; stilts are the mission's signature
(and free S1 gameplay).

LEVEL 1 - BEACH HUT ROW                     [brick | S3 | grid 14x6, 5 layers]
    Three tiny huts side by side, different roof shapes (flat, stepped,
    peaked), each raised one row on short stilt blocks. ~110 entries.
    Collapse story: three separate toy targets; knocking a hut off its
    stilts slides it into its neighbour. Lesson: choose targets.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel beach huts, three small cube-block cabins
    with different roofs standing on short stilts in a row, sandy brick
    tones, white background"

LEVEL 2 - LIFEGUARD TOWER                   [brick | S1 | grid 8x11, 6 layers]
    Small cabin with a peaked roof on four tall slim legs, a 1-cell-wide
    stair ramp leaning against it. ~130 entries.
    Collapse story: the mission's first pure leg-cut - two legs down and the
    cabin belly-flops; the ramp is a bonus domino that falls separately.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel lifeguard tower, small hut with peaked
    roof on four tall thin legs, long ramp leaning to the ground, sandy
    brick cubes, white background"

LEVEL 3 - THE PIER                          [brick | S2 | grid 10x7, 11 layers]
    A boardwalk running AWAY from the player: 4 pile bents (frames of two
    posts + crossbeam) one behind another carrying a 1-row deck, a tiny
    kiosk at the far end. ~160 entries.
    Collapse story: the depth-domino level - fell the front bent and the
    deck pancakes bent by bent all the way to the kiosk.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel wooden pier running into the distance,
    post-and-beam frames carrying a plank deck, small kiosk at the far end,
    brick-brown cubes, white background"

LEVEL 4 - BEACH BAR                   [brick+glass | S4 | grid 12x7, 7 layers]
    Open-front bar: heavy flat roof carried by the back wall and two front
    posts, glass wind-screens filling the sides, loose bottle blocks on the
    counter. ~170 entries.
    Collapse story: shoot a front post and the whole roof slaps down onto
    the counter; the glass screens pop as it comes.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel beach bar, open front with flat roof on
    two posts, glass panels on the sides, counter with small blocks on it,
    sandy bricks and blue glass cubes, white background"

LEVEL 5 - SEAVIEW RESTAURANT          [brick+glass | S4 | grid 12x9, 7 layers]
    Two-storey block: solid brick ground floor, ONE FULL GLASS STOREY
    (panorama dining room), brick roof deck with loose parasol stubs. ~200.
    Collapse story: the glass-waist weak seam by the sea - break the dining
    row and the roof deck drops through it.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel seaside restaurant, brick ground floor,
    entire middle storey of blue glass, rooftop terrace with small blocks,
    cube construction, white background"

LEVEL 6 - WATERSLIDE TOWER            [brick+glass | S3 | grid 14x11, 7 layers]
    A stair tower with a glass-tube slide (stepped diagonal run of glass
    cells) descending to a splash pool rim; separate ticket kiosk. ~220.
    Collapse story: the slide is a cantilevered glass ribbon - cut its two
    support posts and the whole tube rains down the tower's flank.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel water slide tower, brick stair tower with
    a stepped diagonal blue glass tube sliding down to a small pool, ticket
    kiosk beside it, cube blocks, white background"

LEVEL 7 - LIGHTHOUSE            [all three | S1 | grid 9x14, 6 layers]
    Concrete rock base, tapering brick shaft (footprint steps in twice),
    glass lantern room, loose concrete gallery caps. Keeper's cottage
    attached at the foot. ~230 entries.
    Collapse story: the tallest topple in the mission - weaken one side of
    the shaft and the lantern rides the whole tower down onto the cottage.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel lighthouse, grey stone base, tapering
    brick tower, glass lantern room on top, small cottage at the foot, cube
    blocks, white background"

LEVEL 8 - SEA FORT                [all three | S4 | grid 12x10, 8 layers]
    Squat concrete casemate on a stone plinth, brick barracks inside the
    open court, glass observation slit band, loose battlement caps. ~270.
    Collapse story: anatomy - concrete shell resists, so the player learns
    to shoot through the slit band and gut the brick interior first.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel coastal fort, low grey concrete bastion
    with a horizontal glass slit, brick buildings inside the courtyard,
    cube blocks, white background"

LEVEL 9 - BOARDWALK RESORT (BOSS) [all three | S3+S1 | grid 16x12, 10 layers]
    The strip in one map: lifeguard tower (S1 legs) + beach bar (S4 roof) +
    a short pier stub (S2 bents) sharing the beach. ~340 entries.
    Collapse story: graduation - three structures, three styles, and the
    pier deck can be dropped onto the bar's roof.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel beach resort scene, lifeguard tower on
    legs, open beach bar, short wooden pier, sandy bricks blue glass grey
    stone cubes, white background"

4. MISSION 3 - SPACEPORT / ORBITAL FAMILY
================================================================================

Theme: a planetary spaceport - landed ships, domes, gantries, station
modules on cradles. HARD RULE: the engine has gravity and no map may float,
so every "space" structure is GROUNDED - landed, docked, or on pedestal
cradles. That constraint is the mission's gameplay: almost everything
stands on legs, and legs are for cutting. Palette carries the theme:
concrete grey = hull and armour, glass blue = domes and viewports, brick =
painted cargo modules and scaffolds.

LEVEL 1 - CARGO HANGAR                      [brick | S4 | grid 14x8, 7 layers]
    Barrel-profile hangar (stepped arch roof), one huge door opening filling
    most of the front, stacked loose crate blocks inside. ~140 entries.
    Collapse story: the arch IS the building - chip a haunch and the roof
    ring collapses into the crate stack.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel spaceport hangar, stepped arch roof over
    a huge door opening, stacked cargo crates inside, painted brick cubes,
    white background"

LEVEL 2 - LAUNCH GANTRY                     [brick | S2 | grid 10x13, 6 layers]
    A tall open scaffold tower (ladder-like frame, many gaps) beside a
    simple stacked rocket silhouette; two umbilical arms bridge the gap.
    ~160 entries.
    Collapse story: fell the gantry INTO the rocket - the mission opens
    with its own little disaster movie. Arms are cantilever bonuses.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel launch tower, tall open scaffold frame
    next to a simple stacked rocket, two arms connecting them, brick cubes,
    white background"

LEVEL 3 - ROCKET ON LEGS                    [brick | S1 | grid 9x13, 7 layers]
    A fat stacked rocket (3-step taper, fins as buttresses) standing on
    three 2-row legs over a launch ring. ~170 entries.
    Collapse story: pure leg-cut with the tallest payoff so far; the taper
    means the fall sheds loose nose blocks all the way down.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel cartoon rocket standing on three legs
    over a launch ring, stepped tapering body with fins, brick cubes, white
    background"

LEVEL 4 - OBSERVATORY                 [brick+glass | S4 | grid 11x10, 8 layers]
    Drum tower with a stepped glass dome cap and a glass slit down the
    dome's front; attached low lab annex. ~200 entries.
    Collapse story: the dome is a glass avalanche waiting for its drum rim
    to be broken - one clean shot under the cap empties the whole bowl.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel observatory, round tower with a stepped
    blue glass dome and a vertical slit, small annex building, cube blocks,
    white background"

LEVEL 5 - BIODOME PAIR                [brick+glass | S3 | grid 15x8, 8 layers]
    Two stepped glass domes of different sizes on brick ring foundations,
    linked by a glass tube corridor. ~220 entries.
    Collapse story: two shatter targets and a fragile bridge; felling the
    big dome's ring drags the corridor out of the small one.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel greenhouse domes, two stepped blue glass
    domes on brick ring bases connected by a glass tube corridor, cube
    blocks, white background"

LEVEL 6 - CONTROL TOWER               [brick+glass | S1 | grid 9x13, 7 layers]
    Airport-style mushroom tower: slim brick stem, flared deck, wrap-around
    glass cab on top, loose antenna blocks. ~200 entries.
    Collapse story: the definitive stem-cut - the glass cab rides the deck
    down and detonates on the pad.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel control tower, thin brick stem with a
    wider deck on top wrapped in blue glass, antennas above, cube blocks,
    white background"

LEVEL 7 - LANDED SHUTTLE        [all three | S1 | grid 14x9, 8 layers]
    A grey concrete fuselage (stepped nose, stub wings as cantilevers) on
    four slim landing legs, glass cockpit band, brick cargo pods slung
    under the wings. ~260 entries.
    Collapse story: cut the legs and the ship belly-flops, wings snap at
    the root, cockpit glass sprays - the mission's poster moment.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel landed spaceship, grey blocky fuselage
    with stub wings on four thin landing legs, blue glass cockpit stripe,
    brick cargo pods under the wings, cube blocks, white background"

LEVEL 8 - STATION SEGMENT YARD  [all three | S4 | grid 15x10, 8 layers]
    A station core cylinder resting on two concrete cradle stands, radial
    brick modules docked to it, two thin arms carrying flat glass solar
    panels (cantilevered). ~300 entries.
    Collapse story: anatomy + cantilever - pop the solar wings for cheap
    glass rain, then break a cradle and the whole core rolls apart.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel space station module in a ground
    assembly yard, grey cylinder core on two cradle stands, brick side
    modules, thin arms holding flat blue solar panels, cube blocks, white
    background"

LEVEL 9 - THE SPACEPORT (BOSS)  [all three | everything | grid 16x13, 10 layers]
    The whole port: landed shuttle (S1 legs) + control tower (S1 stem) +
    gantry with rocket (S2 domino), sharing one pad. ~380 entries.
    Collapse story: the final exam - three leg-cuts and a domino on one
    map, and the gantry can be dropped across the shuttle's tail.
    IMAGE PROMPT: "isometric voxel spaceport, landed blocky spaceship on
    legs, mushroom control tower with glass cab, scaffold gantry beside a
    small rocket, grey blue and brick cubes, white background"

NOTE - previous families: the industrial and urban high-rise families from
Revision 2 remain valid designs and can return as Missions 4-5; this
revision replaces the themes, not the rules they were built on.

================================================================================
6. RULES FOR THE IMAGE GENERATOR
================================================================================

    I1  CELL RESOLUTION, ALWAYS. One drawn cube = one 0.25 m cell of the
        stated grid. If the drawing shows bricks smaller than the grid, the
        JSON cannot honour it (proven failure - see the render-vs-JSON
        feedback note in Docs).
    I2  CAMERA: isometric-ish, from front-right, elevated ~20-25 degrees -
        the same view as the voxel validation renders, so concept and
        render can be compared side by side.
    I3  PALETTE: brick = warm red-brown, glass = pale sky blue, concrete =
        neutral grey. Nothing else is buildable.
    I4  BACKGROUND: plain white or flat colour. No environment, no people,
        no vehicles - the JSON has no cells for them.
    I5  COUNT WHAT YOU DRAW: the drawn footprint and height must fit the
        stated grid; the stated entry budget is the ceiling.
    I6  ONE IMAGE PER LEVEL, plus (for S2/S1 levels) an optional second
        image showing the intended collapse direction as arrows.

HANDOFF LOOP (per level): image -> approve -> clone to JSON per the
Cloning Handbook -> validate -> voxel render -> compare render to image ->
ship JSON + render + manifest. The render, not the image, is the truth.
