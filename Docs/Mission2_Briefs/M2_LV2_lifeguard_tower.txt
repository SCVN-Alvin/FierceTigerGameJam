THE PITCH
    A small cabin with a peaked roof standing on four tall thin legs, with a long
    ramp leaning up to its door. The mission's first pure leg-cut, and the level
    that teaches the whole Mission 2 lesson in one shot.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
    map id        m2_02_lifeguard_tower
    grid          8 wide x 11 tall x 6 layers
    entries       ~130
    materials     brick only
    band          mid (120-220)
    loose bodies  <= 30

THE SUPPORT TARGET   <-- the reason this level exists
    Two legs on the same side. The cabin belly-flops off its remaining legs and
    lands flat. The ramp is a separate body and comes down on its own a beat
    later - a free second thud.

BUILD RECIPE
    - Legs: four columns at the corners of the cabin footprint, rows y0-y5, one
      cell wide, each its OWN wall (6 cells). They will render as smooth
      stanchions, which is exactly the look wanted.
    - Cabin floor: row y6. MUST include the four cells directly on top of the leg
      tops. This is the level's one non-negotiable geometry rule.
    - Cabin body: rows y7-y9, one cell thick, hollow, with a doorway 1 wide x 2
      tall facing the ramp and a 1-wide window on the seaward side.
    - Peaked roof: row y10, a ridge narrower than the body so the silhouette
      comes to a point.
    - Ramp: ONE CELL DEEP in z. A stepped diagonal run from the ground up to the
      doorway, authored as 2-3 short walls, each footed on the ground or on the
      step below it. It leans against the tower but must not depend on it.
    - Loose: 3-5 single bricks - a flag block on the roof, a buoy at the foot.

COATING FLAGS
    brick    armoured - leave the flag out entirely

PANEL / WELD INTENT
    The four legs are 1x6 solid rectangles - they render smooth, and should.
    The cabin has openings so it welds. Leave both alone.

WATCH OUT
    - The concept image is already reviewed and approved at v2. Two orders carry
      over from that review: the legs are honestly 6 rows tall in the grid, and
      the ramp is one cell deep, not a solid wedge.
    - The ramp must not be a floating diagonal. Every ramp segment needs a cell
      under it - either the ground or the previous step. A ramp hanging off the
      cabin will never fall and will block the 100% clear.
    - Do not thicken the legs to two cells "for stability". Thin legs are the
      point; the physics does not need them.

IMAGE PROMPT (render at 1 brick = 1 cell of the grid above)
    "isometric voxel lifeguard tower, small cube-block hut with a peaked roof
    standing on four tall thin single-block legs, a stepped ramp leaning up to the
    door, sandy brick cubes, chunky blocks, plain white background"
