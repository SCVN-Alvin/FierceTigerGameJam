THE PITCH
    The whole strip in one map: a lifeguard tower on legs, an open beach bar, and
    a short pier stub, sharing one beach. Graduation - three structures, three of
    the mission's styles, and they can be made to hit each other.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
    map id        m2_09_boardwalk_resort
    grid          16 wide x 12 tall x 10 layers
    entries       ~340
    materials     brick + glass + concrete
    band          boss (280-400)
    loose bodies  <= 80

THE SUPPORT TARGET   <-- the reason this level exists
    Three of them, and they interact. The pier deck can be dropped onto the bar's
    roof; the lifeguard tower can be felled across the pier. The intended solve is
    an ORDER, not a total: fell the tower onto the pier, the pier onto the bar,
    and one shot's worth of damage cascades across the whole beach.

BUILD RECIPE
    - Lifeguard tower at x0-x4: four 1xN legs, cabin, peaked roof. Same recipe as
      LV2, shrunk one row.
    - Beach bar at x6-x11: back wall, two front posts, heavy roof, glass screens.
      Same recipe as LV4, shrunk.
    - Pier stub at x12-x15 running into z: two bents and a short deck. Same recipe
      as LV3, two bents instead of four.
    - Concrete: a low breakwater ring at y0 along the seaward edge, tying the
      scene together and giving the boss its third material.
    - Position them so the tower's fall line crosses the pier and the pier's deck
      overhangs the bar's roof. Measure this - it is the level's whole design.
    - Loose: 12-20 across the three structures.

COATING FLAGS
    brick     armoured - leave the flag out entirely
    concrete  armoured - leave the flag out entirely
    glass     "bare": true on every glass wall

PANEL / WELD INTENT
    Legs, posts and piles smooth; decks, walls and roofs welded. Nothing new.

WATCH OUT
    - BUDGET IS THE REAL ENEMY HERE. 340 entries and 80 loose across three
      structures, on a 16x12x10 grid - that is the ceiling on every axis at once.
      Build the three structures at Mission-2-minus-one-size, not full size.
    - Three structures means three wall families. NOTHING may weld across the gaps
      between them, or the cascade becomes one dead slab.
    - Verify the fall lines in Smash Builder before delivering. If the tower does
      not actually reach the pier, the boss is just three small levels in a row.
    - This is the last Mission 2 map. It should feel like a summary, not like a
      new idea.
