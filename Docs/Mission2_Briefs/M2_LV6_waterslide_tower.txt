THE PITCH
    A stair tower with a glass tube slide spiralling down from the top to a splash
    pool rim, with a ticket kiosk beside it. The most sculptural silhouette in the
    mission and the one with the most ways to go wrong.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
    map id        m2_06_waterslide_tower
    grid          14 wide x 11 tall x 7 layers
    entries       ~220
    materials     brick + glass
    band          hard (220-350)
    loose bodies  <= 50

THE SUPPORT TARGET   <-- the reason this level exists
    The slide's props. Cut two of them and the whole glass ribbon lets go and rains
    down the tower's flank in sequence. The tower itself survives, which is the
    point - this level rewards attacking the thing hanging off the building rather
    than the building.

BUILD RECIPE
    - Tower: x0-x4, rows y0-y10, hollow shell one cell thick, with 1-wide window
      slits (NOT 2-wide, see the guide 4.2) and an internal stair of stepped brick
      cells.
    - Slide: a stepped diagonal run of glass cells from (x5,y9) descending to
      (x13,y1). One cell wide, one cell deep. Group it into 3-4 walls of 4-8 cells
      so it comes apart in sections rather than all at once.
    - PROPS: a brick column under the slide every 2-3 cells of its run, rows from
      the ground up to the slide. Each prop is its OWN 1xN wall. There must be a
      slide cell sitting DIRECTLY on top of each prop.
    - Pool rim: a low brick ring at y0-y1 around x10-x13, catching the slide's end.
    - Kiosk: a separate 3x3x3 hut at the far side, unconnected to the tower.
    - Loose: 5-8 single bricks - signage, buckets on the pool rim.

COATING FLAGS
    brick    armoured - leave the flag out entirely
    glass    "bare": true on every glass wall

PANEL / WELD INTENT
    Props are 1xN rectangles - smooth, correct.
    The slide is a diagonal so it can never be a rectangle; it welds and shows
    individual glass segments, which reads as a tube. Correct.

WATCH OUT
    THIS IS THE HIGHEST-RISK LEVEL IN THE MISSION FOR HANGING BODIES.
    - A slide attached only to the tower's flank is held laterally and will NEVER
      be released. It will hang in the air for the rest of the run and make the
      100% clear impossible. Props are not decoration; they are the mechanism.
    - Every slide wall must contain at least one cell directly above a prop.
      Verify per wall, not per level.
    - Prove this level in the Smash Builder "Review gameplay" tab before
      delivering. Cut the props, watch the tube come down, confirm the map can
      reach 100%. Do not ship it unplayed.
    - 14 x 11 x 7 with a tower and a kiosk runs hot on entries. Hollow everything.

IMAGE PROMPT (render at 1 brick = 1 cell of the grid above)
    "isometric voxel water slide tower, tall brick stair tower with narrow slit
    windows, a stepped diagonal pale blue glass tube descending from the top down
    to a low pool rim, thin brick props holding the tube up along its run, small
    ticket kiosk to the side, chunky cube blocks, plain white background"
