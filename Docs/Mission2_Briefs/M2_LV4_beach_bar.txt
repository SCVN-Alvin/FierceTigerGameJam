THE PITCH
    An open-fronted bar: a heavy flat roof carried by the back wall and just two
    slim front posts, glass wind-screens closing the sides, bottles lined up on
    the counter. The first appearance of glass in the mission, and the first
    heavy-lid drop.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
    map id        m2_04_beach_bar
    grid          12 wide x 7 tall x 7 layers
    entries       ~170
    materials     brick + glass
    band          mid (120-220)
    loose bodies  <= 40

THE SUPPORT TARGET   <-- the reason this level exists
    One front post. The roof loses a corner, tips forward, and slaps down onto the
    counter - taking the glass screens with it on the way. A single shot for the
    whole front half of the building.

BUILD RECIPE
    - Back wall: x0-x11 at z6, rows y0-y4, one cell thick, with a 2x2 opening as a
      back door.
    - Front posts: two columns at (x2,z0) and (x9,z0), rows y0-y4, one cell wide,
      each its OWN wall. Nothing else holds the front.
    - Roof: rows y5-y6 spanning x0-x11 and z0-z6. Heavy on purpose - it is the
      thing that falls.
    - Glass wind-screens: at x0 and x11, z1-z5, rows y1-y4. Group each screen as
      one wall so it releases in one go.
    - Counter: row y2, a run of brick from x3 to x8 at z4, with the bottles on it.
    - Loose: 6-10 single bricks as bottles on the counter row y3, plus 2-3 stools.

COATING FLAGS
    brick    armoured - leave the flag out entirely
    glass    "bare": true on every glass wall

PANEL / WELD INTENT
    The two front posts are 1x5 rectangles - smooth, correct.
    The glass screens are single-layer rectangles and will render smooth - correct
    for glazing, leave them.
    The roof spans seven layers so it welds and shows planks - correct.

WATCH OUT
    - The roof must NOT be welded into the same wall as the back wall. If it is,
      cutting a front post cannot tip it. Roof and back wall are separate bodies
      that merely touch.
    - The roof needs cells directly over both front posts, or it is held laterally
      and will hang when the posts go.
    - Bottles are loose 1x1 bricks, not a wall. They are the crown debris of this
      level and should scatter individually.
