THE PITCH
    A squat concrete casemate on a stone plinth with a horizontal glass observation
    slit, brick barracks standing in the open court inside. The mission's one
    anatomy level: there is no leg to cut, so the player has to learn where the
    building is soft.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
    map id        m2_08_sea_fort
    grid          12 wide x 10 tall x 8 layers
    entries       ~270
    materials     brick + glass + concrete
    band          hard (220-350)
    loose bodies  <= 60

THE SUPPORT TARGET   <-- the reason this level exists
    None - deliberately. The way in is the glass slit band. Shoot through it at a
    shallow angle, gut the brick barracks inside, and the concrete shell is left
    standing over nothing. This is the level that teaches "look for the opening".

BUILD RECIPE
    - Plinth: rows y0-y1, concrete, the full footprint.
    - Casemate shell: rows y2-y7, concrete, perimeter only, one cell thick,
      enclosing an open court. No roof over the court.
    - Slit band: rows y4-y5 on the front and side faces, glass, continuous. This
      is the only way into the interior and it must be wide enough to shoot
      through at an angle - do not break it up with concrete mullions.
    - Barracks: two brick huts inside the court, 3x3x3 each, standing free on the
      plinth, not touching the shell.
    - Battlements: row y8-y9, loose concrete caps alternating along the top.
    - Loose: 8-12 caps and rubble blocks.

COATING FLAGS
    brick     armoured - leave the flag out entirely
    concrete  armoured - leave the flag out entirely
    glass     "bare": true on every glass wall

PANEL / WELD INTENT
    The concrete faces are large single-layer rectangles and will render smooth -
    correct, a casemate should look poured, and it contrasts with the blocky
    brick barracks inside. This is the one level where the panel/weld difference
    is doing narrative work: hard smooth shell, soft blocky interior.

WATCH OUT
    - The shell must not seal the interior. If a Rock player can see no path to
      the barracks, the level is unwinnable on starting gear AND unreadable. Test
      the slit's sightlines.
    - Barracks must be separate bodies from the shell and from each other.
    - Concrete armoured plus concrete's 6 HP makes this the tankiest level so far.
      Keep the shell one cell thick and hollow the plinth's interior.
    - Verify the ammunition budget against Cannon Lv1, not Rock - see LV7's note.

IMAGE PROMPT (render at 1 brick = 1 cell of the grid above)
