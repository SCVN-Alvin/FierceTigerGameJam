THE PITCH
    A boardwalk running away from the player into the sea: four pile bents one
    behind another carrying a plank deck, with a small kiosk at the far end. The
    depth level - the only one in the mission that uses the z axis as its subject.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
    map id        m2_03_the_pier
    grid          10 wide x 7 tall x 11 layers
    entries       ~160
    materials     brick only
    band          mid (120-220)
    loose bodies  <= 40

THE SUPPORT TARGET   <-- the reason this level exists
    The front bent. With it gone the deck's near end drops, and the deck pancakes
    bent by bent all the way to the kiosk. One shot, four collapses.

BUILD RECIPE
    - Bents at z0, z3, z6, z9. Each bent = two posts (rows y0-y3, one cell wide,
      at x3 and x6) plus a crossbeam at y4 joining them. Each post is its OWN
      wall; the crossbeam belongs to the bent, not to the deck.
    - Deck: row y5, spanning x2-x7 across every layer z0-z10. The deck cells at
      z0, z3, z6 and z9 sit directly over the posts - that is what lets each bent
      release its share when it goes.
    - Railings: row y6 at x2 and x7, broken into 2-3 cell segments so they rain
      down separately.
    - Kiosk: a 3x3 hut at the far end, z9-z10, rows y6-y8, hollow, with a serving
      hatch. It rides the last bent down.
    - Loose: 5-8 single bricks - crates and mooring blocks along the deck.

COATING FLAGS
    brick    armoured - leave the flag out entirely

PANEL / WELD INTENT
    Posts are 1x4 rectangles and render smooth - correct for piles.
    The deck spans eleven layers so it welds and shows planks - correct.

WATCH OUT
    THIS IS THE MOST LIKELY LEVEL IN THE MISSION TO PRODUCE A FLOATING DECK.
    - Every deck segment must have a cell directly over a post. A deck grouped as
      one 11-layer wall is fine; a deck split into per-bay walls is fine only if
      each bay contains a cell over a post. Check this cell by cell.
    - Do not group the deck and the crossbeams into one wall. If the deck is the
      same body as the bents, cutting a post does nothing until the whole thing
      dies at once, and the pancake never happens.
    - Depth 11 layers is close to the 12 ceiling. Keep the deck narrow (6 cells)
      so the entry count stays in band.
