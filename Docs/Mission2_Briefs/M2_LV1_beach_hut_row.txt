THE PITCH
    Three tiny huts standing side by side on short stilts, each with a different
    roof - flat, stepped, peaked. The mission's opening statement: these are not
    one building, they are three toys, and you choose which one to knock over.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
    map id        m2_01_beach_hut_row
    grid          14 wide x 6 tall x 5 layers
    entries       ~110
    materials     brick only
    band          tutorial (50-120)
    loose bodies  <= 30

THE SUPPORT TARGET   <-- the reason this level exists
    Any single hut's two stilts on one side. The hut tips off its legs and slides
    into its neighbour, which starts the neighbour going. Two shots should be able
    to start a three-hut chain if aimed in the right order.

BUILD RECIPE
    - Hut A occupies x0-x3, hut B x5-x8, hut C x10-x13. Leave x4 and x9 EMPTY -
      the gaps are what make them three targets instead of one wall.
    - Stilts: rows y0-y1. Four short posts per hut, one at each corner of its
      footprint, each post authored as its OWN wall (2 cells, 1 wide).
    - Floor: row y2, and it MUST include the four cells sitting directly on the
      post tops. Without those the hut is held only from the side and will hang.
    - Body: rows y3-y4, one cell thick, hollow. One doorway 1 wide x 2 tall per
      hut, on different faces so the three read differently.
    - Roofs at y5, one per hut: flat slab / stepped (two cells then one) / peaked
      (a single ridge cell). This is the whole visual variety of the level.
    - Loose: 4-8 single bricks as crates and buoys on the sand between the huts.

COATING FLAGS
    brick    armoured - leave the flag out entirely
    (no glass, no concrete on this level)

PANEL / WELD INTENT
    Stilts are 1x2 solid rectangles and will render smooth - correct, they read as
    timber posts. Hut bodies have doorways so they weld and show blocks - correct.
    Nothing to force either way.

WATCH OUT
    - The optimizer must NOT weld two huts into one body across the x4 / x9 gaps.
      Check the wall list: no wall may contain cells from two different huts.
    - A 2-cell stilt wall must not have its two bricks merged into one brick_2x1;
      that leaves a one-block wall, which the engine demotes to a loose block.
    - Keep it small. This is the tutorial band; 110 entries, not 160.

IMAGE PROMPT (render at 1 brick = 1 cell of the grid above)
    "isometric voxel beach huts, three small cube-block cabins side by side with
    different roofs - one flat, one stepped, one peaked - each raised on four
    short stilts above flat sand, sandy brick tones, chunky cube blocks, plain
    white background, no ground texture"
