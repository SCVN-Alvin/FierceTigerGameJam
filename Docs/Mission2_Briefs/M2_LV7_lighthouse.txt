THE PITCH
    A concrete rock base, a tapering brick shaft that steps in twice as it climbs,
    a glass lantern room at the top, and a keeper's cottage at the foot. The
    tallest topple in the game, and the mission's first concrete.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
    map id        m2_07_lighthouse
    grid          9 wide x 14 tall x 6 layers
    entries       ~230
    materials     brick + glass + concrete
    band          hard (220-350)
    loose bodies  <= 50

THE SUPPORT TARGET   <-- the reason this level exists
    One side of the shaft, low down. Weaken it and the entire tower rides over,
    lantern and all, and lands on the cottage. The cottage is destroyed by the
    tower, not by the player.

BUILD RECIPE
    - Base: rows y0-y2, concrete, a squat plinth wider than the shaft. Read the
      Cannon-gating note below before deciding its size.
    - Shaft: rows y3-y11, brick, hollow, one cell thick. Footprint steps in by one
      cell at y6 and again at y9 - each ledge is a debris shelf on the way down.
      Band the shaft into storeys of 3 rows so it fails floor by floor.
    - Lantern room: rows y12-y13, glass all round, with a loose concrete gallery
      cap ring at y13 that rains down separately.
    - Cottage: a 3x3x3 brick hut at the foot on the side the tower will fall
      towards. Do not connect it to the base - it must be a separate body so it is
      crushed rather than co-destroyed.
    - Loose: 6-10 gallery caps and rocks around the base.

COATING FLAGS
    brick     armoured - leave the flag out entirely
    concrete  armoured FROM THIS LEVEL ON - leave the flag out
    glass     "bare": true on every glass wall

PANEL / WELD INTENT
    The concrete plinth faces are likely to be solid single-layer rectangles and
    will render smooth - correct, it reads as poured stone.
    The tapering shaft has openings and steps, so it welds. Correct.

WATCH OUT
    FIRST CANNON-GATED LEVEL - DECIDE THIS DELIBERATELY AND WRITE IT DOWN.
    Rock does 0 damage to concrete at every level, not slow damage - zero. A
    player on the starting ammunition cannot remove one concrete cell by shooting.
    Two honest options:
      a) Keep the base small (2-3 rows, tight footprint) and rely on the falling
         shaft to smash it. The level is then clearable on Rock, hard but fair.
      b) Make the base substantial and accept that the level cannot be 100%-ed
         until the Cannon is bought. That is a legitimate paywall-free unlock, but
         it must be a decision, not an accident.
    State which one in the manifest.
    - The shaft must actually topple, not crumble in place. Storey banding plus a
      hollow core is what makes it fall as a tower.
    - 14 rows is the tallest grid in the mission. Keep the footprint small (9
      wide) or the entry count runs away.
